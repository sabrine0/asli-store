Before you ask · ⵉⵙⵇⵙⵉⵜⵏ

Frequently asked.

We ship worldwide via DHL Express from Marrakech. USA & Canada: 2–4 business days ($18). Europe & UK: 2–3 days ($18). Asia & Pacific: 3–5 days ($28). Shipping is free on every order over $150. You’ll receive a tracking number as soon as your piece leaves the workshop.

We offer 30-day returns on unused items in original condition. Because most pieces are made to order by hand, we ask that you contact us first so we can arrange the return with the artisan in mind. Custom and personalised pieces are final sale.

Yes — every single one. We meet each artisan in person, pay them 2–3× the local market rate, and put their name and region on the product page. Slight variations in colour, weave, and finish are the signature of genuine handwork, not flaws.

Absolutely. We love commissions — a specific rug size, a colour, a set of poufs for a riad or restaurant. Choose “Custom / commission” or “Wholesale & trade” in the form above and tell us what you have in mind. Lead times vary by craft (rugs can take 30–90 days).

Care depends on the craft. Leather: wipe with a damp cloth, condition twice a year. Rugs: shake out, spot-clean, professional clean only. Brass: buff with a soft cloth. Tagines: season before first use. Each product page lists specific care, and we include a care card with every order.
