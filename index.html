Our story · ⴰⵎⵣⵔⵓⵢ

Aṣli means real.
And nothing is more real
than this.

We started in a small Casablanca apartment with one belief — the world deserves to see the real Morocco. Not the airport-souvenir version, but the slow craft passed down through generations of Amazigh, Berber, and Arab artisans.

Every piece is sourced directly from the makers — most of them women from cooperatives in the Atlas Mountains. No middlemen, no warehouses, no compromise. Just authentic craft, made the way it has been made for centuries — by hand.

Meet the makers · ⵉⵎⵙⵏⴰⵍⵏ

The hands behind every piece.

Fatima has been weaving Beni Ourain rugs since she was seven, taught by her grandmother in the Aït Bouguemez valley high in the Atlas. Each of her rugs takes between 30 and 90 days to complete — a single mistake means starting over.

When you buy one of my rugs, you are not buying a thing. You are buying a year of my life, my mother's stories, my daughter's future. — Fatima Z., Aït Bouguemez
